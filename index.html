Five changes worth flagging.

The Smart Bed tracks weight, breathing rate, heart rate and sleep while your dog rests. See real-time health alerts in the WoafyPet app on your phone when a change may signal pain or stress.

Joints

Stiffness after naps can be a sign of arthritis.

Weight

Extra weight puts more strain on sore joints.

Breathing

Faster breathing during sleep can signal heart or lung trouble.

Heart rate

Pain or stress can raise their resting heart rate.

Sleep

Repeated waking can be a sign of discomfort.
