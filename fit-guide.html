How do I choose a bed for my senior dog?

Sleeping space
Measure from nose to back paws when your dog stretches out for a nap. Compare that length with the usable mattress space inside the bolsters.
Living-room fit
Mark the bed’s outer dimensions on the floor beside their favorite spot. Leave a clear path so they can walk in and turn around.
Cover care
For muddy paws or a small spill, wipe the cover. For a full wash, unzip the WoafyPet cover and machine wash it according to its care label. Keep the foam and Smart Base out of the washer.
