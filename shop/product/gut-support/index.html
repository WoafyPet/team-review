Start with the bowl they know

For an older dog with a well-established appetite, mealtime is a natural place to build digestive care into the day. Keep the bowl and feeding time familiar.

The next walk tells you more

A formed stool that’s easy to pick up is a practical sign of normal digestion. Notice changes in firmness and how often your dog needs to go out, especially after a change in food.

Keep the rest of dinner familiar

Use the same food and treats as you introduce digestive support. A sudden menu change can upset digestion and make it hard to tell which change your dog tolerated.

Daily serving

Use the daily amount printed on the WoafyPet package. If your dog follows a prescribed diet, check the ingredient panel against that diet before adding the supplement.
