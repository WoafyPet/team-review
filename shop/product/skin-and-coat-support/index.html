Care for the skin under the fur

Nutrition helps maintain the skin barrier that holds moisture in. At brushing time, part the fur behind the ears and under the collar to see whether the skin looks smooth or flaky.

Notice changes between baths

Run a hand down their back after towel-drying. New flakes, a rough patch or repeated licking in one spot are easier to spot when you know what their coat normally feels like.

Pair nutrition with regular brushing

Brushing works through loose hair at the surface. Food supplies the nutrients for the skin and hair growing underneath. Keep both parts of the routine consistent.

Daily serving

Measure the daily amount listed on your WoafyPet package for your dog. Compare the ingredients with any coat or fish-oil supplements they already take before combining them.
