BEFORE YOU ORDER

Shipping & returns

Free shipping over $75 · U.S.

Choose your country and region at checkout to see the shipping charge and complete order total before payment. Standard international shipping is available to supported destinations.

Free standard shipping applies to orders over $75 after discounts, delivered within all 50 United States and Washington, D.C., including Alaska and Hawaii. U.S. territories, military addresses and international destinations are excluded from this offer. Taxes do not count toward the qualifying subtotal.

For orders of $75 or less, contiguous U.S. shipping is $6.95 for the first billable pound, plus $3.00 per additional pound. Alaska and Hawaii shipping is $14.95 for the first billable pound, plus $3.95 per additional pound. Fractions round up to the next pound; bulky items may use volumetric weight.

International shipping varies by product, size, quantity and destination. Each unit receives a shipping charge, and items may arrive in separate parcels. These are our store shipping charges, not live carrier quotes. The charge shown before payment is the shipping amount you pay; we will not increase it after purchase.

Import duties, destination taxes and customs charges are not included and may be collected by your destination. Service suspensions, restricted destinations and parcels without an available rate require a quote. Contact hello@woafmeow.com if checkout cannot calculate shipping for your order.

Processing & dispatch

Allow 48–72 hours for order processing.

Customized products ship out within 10–14 days after successful payment and receipt of complete customization details. This customization dispatch window includes order processing; it is not an additional 10–14 days on top of the 48–72 hours.

Dispatch is not delivery. Carrier transit time is additional. We will email delivery updates. If we cannot ship within the promised time, we will notify you and offer the choice of accepting a revised date or cancelling unshipped merchandise for a refund, as required by law.

30-day free returns

Contact us within 30 days of delivery to request a return of an eligible item. It must be unopened, unused, unwashed, unaltered and complete in its original packaging, with original seals intact where supplied, in good condition and suitable for resale. Keep all parts, accessories and tags. Opened or used items, or items with pet hair, odors, damage from use or missing parts, are not eligible for change-of-mind returns.

Email hello@woafmeow.com with your order number and photos of the item and packaging. Once approved, we provide a prepaid return label: no return-shipping or restocking fee for an eligible return. Please use our label and instructions rather than sending an item back without approval.

Customized products are final sale as described below. Opened chews, wipes and refills cannot be resold and are not eligible for change-of-mind returns. These conditions do not limit remedies for faulty, damaged or incorrect items, or rights required by applicable law.

Customized products are final sale

We do not accept change-of-mind returns, exchanges or refunds for customized products. This includes personalization, names, photos and made-to-order customization selected for your order. Check all spelling, images, measurements, sizes and selections before paying.

This restriction does not remove your rights if an item is faulty, damaged, incorrect or materially not as described, or any cancellation or refund right required by applicable law.

Damaged, faulty or incorrect items

Tell us promptly if your order arrives damaged, faulty or incorrect. Keep the packaging and share photos of the item, packaging and shipping label so we can investigate. We will arrange the appropriate remedy, including replacement or refund where required. Do not discard or return the item until we provide instructions.

Refunds & return shipping

We inspect returned items against the conditions above and issue approved refunds to the original payment method. Your bank or payment provider determines when the credit appears. Eligible 30-day returns use our prepaid label with no return-shipping or restocking fee. For a faulty, damaged or incorrect item, we provide the appropriate return instructions and remedy without limiting your statutory rights.
