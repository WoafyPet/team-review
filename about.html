FROM OUR FAMILY

Bobby’s story

Bobby was three weeks old when he came home with me. For eight years, he was my best friend. I look at this photo of the two of us and wish I could reach back in and hold him again.

RobBobby’s dad & founder of WoafyPet

The boy who waited for me.

Whatever kind of day I’d had at school, Bobby was there at the door when I got home. He was so happy just to see me. As a kid, I took it for granted that tomorrow I’d open the door and find him there again.

When I miss him now, that’s the moment I want back. A regular school day, and my boy there at the end of it.

I wish you could have told me.

Bobby, for over a year we thought your pain was arthritis. It was bone cancer. You were eight when I lost you. You had spent so much of your life looking out for me. I wish I’d understood sooner what you needed from me.

Dogs are good at hiding pain. I wish I’d known sooner. That’s why WoafyPet exists.
