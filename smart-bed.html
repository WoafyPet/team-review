Know what to flag to your vet.

Check what’s unusual for your dog and share changes with your vet.

Heart rate

An unusually fast or slow resting pulse with weakness can be a clue to an abnormal heart rhythm.

Breathing rate

Faster breathing during sleep with a new cough can point to heart or lung disease.

Sleep

Restless nights with morning stiffness can point to arthritis pain.

Weight

Extra weight can worsen arthritis. Unexplained weight loss can accompany illness.
